# Noxi: voice

## Sentence style
- Short. One idea per line. Plain English, no Latin in the words on screen.
- Talk to the viewer as "you" and their friends as "your crew".
- A desire line first: what the viewer wants (to remember the night, to have the photos), not what is wrong.
- source: Noxi repo `CLAUDE.md`, Philip's build prompt, 2026-11-14

## Words to use
- crew, your crew, the night, nights out, one shot, the recap, your look, remember
- source: past Noxi ads (`marketing-videos/src/noxi/`) and the site, 2026-11-14

## Words to avoid
The brain check reports any of these, in any case, with or without a plural s.
- drink, drinking, drunk, booze, beer, wine, cocktail, cocktails, liquor, vodka, tequila, whiskey, rum, champagne, prosecco, sake, cider, margarita, mimosa, hangover, hungover, wasted, blackout, tipsy, buzzed, shotgun, keg, pregame, pregaming, bar crawl, pub crawl, happy hour, last call, bottle service, cheers, chug
- 🍺, 🍻, 🍷, 🍸, 🍹, 🥂, 🥃, 🍾, 🍶, 🧉
- source: Philip's build prompt ("No drinks, no drink words and no drink emoji"), Noxi website rules, 2026-11-14

Note: Noxi calls a photo a "shot" ("one shot, no retakes"). That word is allowed. Never pair it with a drink.
